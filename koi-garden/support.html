How do I play?

Drag a block onto the 9×9 board. Fill a whole row, a whole column or one of the nine 3×3 beds and those stones clear. Tap a block to rotate it. The round ends when none of your blocks fit. Tap ? in the game for the full rules.

What are joker koi?

Big moves earn a joker koi. Clear two lines at once for an Arrow Koi, keep a combo going for a Ripple Koi, or clear three lines (or a same-colour 3×3 bed) for a Rainbow Koi. Drag it onto any empty cell to use it.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads

Open Settings (the gear) and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Optional reward videos stay available; you only see them when you choose to.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress?

No, as long as you are signed in to iCloud. Your progress is saved on your iPhone and in your iCloud, so it comes back when you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone with the same Apple Account. Open the game once with an internet connection, and your levels, koi and streak return.

Can I turn off the daily reminder?

Yes. Open Settings → Daily reminder in the game, or turn off notifications for Koi Garden in the iPhone Settings app.
