How to play

Three ways to clear

Row
Column
3×3 square
  1. Drag a piece onto the board. Tap it to turn it.
  2. Fill a row, column or 3×3 square and it clears.
  3. Clear more at once, or clear again soon after, for bigger points.
  4. Keep space free: the round ends when no piece fits.

Want to know more? Tap a topic.

Points and combos
  • Each stone you place gives 1 point.
  • Clearing 1 line gives 10, 2 at once give 40, 3 at once give 90.
  • Combo: clear again within 3 moves and your points multiply.
  • Harmony: fill a 3×3 square with one colour for +100. Squares close to done glow green, or gold if they are still one colour.
Journey levels
  • Each level has a goal and a set number of pieces.
  • Golden koi stones are freed when their line or square clears.
  • Stones with a gold ring are the ones the goal asks you to clear.
  • Ice (blue frost with a snowflake) needs two clears: one to break it, one to remove the stone. A joker koi takes it in one go.
  • Pieces left over give bonus points and stars. Taking 10 extra pieces from a video gives 1 star at most.
  • Every fifth level is hard and gives double pearls. Each of the first 15 levels you finish adds a koi to your pond.
  • Bamboo (from chapter 2) grows into an empty spot next to it after a few moves without a clear. Every clear resets the count.
  • Lotus flowers (from chapter 3) float on empty spots. Clear a line over one to collect it.
  • Each chapter has 15 levels. Finishing one gives 100 pearls and opens the next, and the journey goes on for as long as you play.
Endless and Daily
  • Endless: the higher your score, the bigger the pieces. From 800 points a rock falls into the pond every 8 moves.
  • Daily: everyone gets the same board and the same 30 pieces. Your first try counts for your streak. Hint, undo and swap are off.
  • Missed a day? Save your streak with pearls or a short video, once a week.
Hint, undo and swap
  • Hint shows a good spot and turns the piece for you.
  • Undo takes back your last move, twice per round.
  • Swap gives you two new pieces.
  • Hint and swap cost pearls, or a short video.
Joker koi
  • Clear 2 lines in one move: Arrow Koi, clears its row and column.
  • Reach combo ×3: Ripple Koi, clears a 5×5 area.
  • Clear 3 lines or a Harmony square: Rainbow Koi, clears every stone in the colour it lands next to.
  • Drag a joker onto any empty cell. While you drag, the board shows what it will clear. Tap a joker to see a small example. You can hold three. Unused jokers give 50 points each when you win a level.
Win streak
  • Win Journey levels in a row to build a win streak.
  • 1 win in a row: the next level starts with an Arrow Koi. 3 in a row: an Arrow and a Ripple Koi. 5 in a row: a Rainbow Koi too.
  • Losing a level ends the current. Keep going with a video and win, and it is saved.
Pond tasks
  • Three small tasks each day, in any mode. New ones arrive at midnight.
  • Finish all three to open a bamboo basket with pearls and a joker for your next Journey level.
  • Clear the whole board at once for Still water: +150 points.
Board & stones
  • Pick a board and a kind of stone with the brush on the home screen, or in Settings.
  • Cherry Blossom opens at 30 stars and Glazed Ceramic at 15. Jade Temple and Gemstones cost 300 pearls each.
  • More boards and stones are for pearls: Bamboo Mat, Snow Garden, Koi Pond and Starry Night, and Hinoki Wood, Marble, Sea Glass and Blue Porcelain stones.
  • Moon Lacquer and Lanterns open with a 14-day Daily streak or a win streak of 5.
  • Every colour keeps its own stone shape in every style.
Weekly festivals
  • A new festival starts every Monday: Lantern Festival, Moon Viewing, Sakura Week and Firefly Night take turns.
  • Each counts a different kind of move, in every mode. Its goals give pearls, and the last one brings the festival’s own koi.
  • After a big move, a lantern, moonbeam, petal or firefly floats up from the board. Tap it for more.
  • Missed a festival koi? The same festival comes back four weeks later.
Surprises in the pond
  • Now and then a lucky golden koi glides over the board. Tap it for pearls.
  • A dragonfly may land on an empty spot. Cover it with a stone within five moves for pearls.
  • Tap the water around the board and the koi dart away.
  • None of them change your score, so every level stays fair.
