Privacy policy

Koi Garden: Block Puzzle · Last updated 23 October 2026

Koi Garden: Block Puzzle ("the app") is made by Kristian Clifford, Norway ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Short version: we do not run any servers and we never see your data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google handles some data to show and measure those ads.

Data stored on your iPhone

Your scores, level progress, koi collection, settings and daily streak are stored only on your device. We cannot access them. You can delete them at any time by deleting the app.

No account, no personal details

The app has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Information about ads you see and tap
  • Diagnostic data, such as crashes and performance of the ad software

Your choices: if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. iOS asks whether the app may track you; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you see non-personalised ads. You can change this later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Read how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your card details or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple whether the purchase has been made.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your iPhone. No data is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention are based on legitimate interests in funding a free app. You have the right to access, correct and delete personal data and to complain to your data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet). As we do not hold data about you, requests about ad data are best directed to Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and publish the new version on this page.
